Privacy Policy

Chan-Chan's Sewing & Alterations ("we", "us", "our") operates a clothing alteration shop at 1 Glen Rd, Suite 207, West Lebanon, NH 03784. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you drop off clothes for alteration, we collect your name, your mobile phone number, and details about your order (such as the garment, the alteration requested, and your work/order number). If you contact us by phone or in person, we may also keep the contact details you give us.

How we use your information

We use your information only to run the shop and serve your order, including:

  • Tracking and completing your alteration order;
  • Contacting you about your order, including text message notifications when your order is completed and ready for pickup;
  • Answering your questions about our services, hours, and location.

Text messages (SMS)

If you agree to receive text messages from us, we will only send you messages about your order status, such as a notification that your order is ready for pickup. We do not send promotional or marketing text messages. You can stop these messages at any time by replying STOP, and reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We share your information only as needed to provide our services (for example, with our phone service provider to deliver the text messages you agreed to receive) or when required by law.
